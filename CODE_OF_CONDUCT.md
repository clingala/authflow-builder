# Code of Conduct

AuthFlow Builder is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and make space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of community work.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
